# Community conduct

Treat contributors respectfully across language, script, dialect, nationality, religion, gender and experience. Give specific, evidence-based feedback. Harassment, threats, doxxing, discrimination and publication of private information are prohibited.

Dataset discussions may describe offensive language for research. Use warnings and avoid reproducing slurs in issues. Never infer an individual's identity from dataset records.

Report concerns to the repository owner using GitHub's available reporting tools. Do not post private evidence in a public issue. Maintainers may hide harmful content, close discussions or restrict participation proportionately; provide an explanation when safe. Appeals may be sent to the owner through an available private GitHub reporting channel. No dedicated private project email is configured yet.
