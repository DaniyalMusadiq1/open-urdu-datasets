# Discovery catalog — unreleased draft

28 upstream resources (27 dataset/corpus resources and one embeddings resource): **2 verified scoped datasets, 2 pending review, 24 unverified**. The target of at least 25 verified datasets is not met. Derived hosted samples are separate and do not inflate this count.

Verified means source, explicit license and technical checks of the stated files, not native annotation accuracy. Belebele scope: complete Urdu Arabic/Latin JSONL variants, aligned to English at a pinned revision. Its 1,800 Urdu variant rows represent the same underlying 900 questions. Dakshina Urdu native-text/lexicon scope now has 622,805 hosted records with 8,948,752 native whitespace tokens; source archive hash, per-record provenance and shard evidence are recorded. All records are locally unreviewed. UCI downloads were checked but annotation anomalies leave them pending review. Other source-reported sizes are not local verification. Entries can overlap; never sum rows as unique examples.
