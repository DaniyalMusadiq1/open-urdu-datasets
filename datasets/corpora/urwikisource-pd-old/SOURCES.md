# Sources and attribution

- **Content source:** [Urdu Wikisource](https://ur.wikisource.org/), exported in the Wikimedia monthly content dump dated 2026-11-01. The exact dump URL and SHA-256 are recorded in `manifest.json`.
- **Rights filter:** [Wikisource copyright tags](https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/WS:CT) documents `{{PD-old}}` as applying to works whose author died over 100 years ago and as worldwide public domain. Only main-namespace pages carrying that exact tag were retained. Other public-domain tags were not treated as sufficient by this importer.
- **Revision provenance:** every record includes its source page, numeric revision ID, timestamp, fixed revision URL and page-history URL.
- **Transcription attribution/license:** see `LICENSE` and the per-record `license` field. The compilation license does not relicense underlying public-domain works.

The dump is a Wikimedia export, not a complete collection of every Urdu book or public-domain work available online. The release makes no completeness claim.
